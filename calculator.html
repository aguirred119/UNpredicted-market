THE FIRST 24 HOURS

Don't make the loss bigger tonight.

You don't have to solve your entire financial situation immediately. The first goal is simply preventing another loss.

01

Stop depositing

Don't borrow, transfer savings or use another credit card to try to recover what was lost.

02

Create friction

Log out, remove stored payment methods, use available account limits or self-exclusion tools, and step away.

03

Tell one person

Secrecy makes it easier to return to the cycle. Consider telling someone you trust what happened.

The money you've already lost doesn't make the next wager more likely to win.

Previous losses are already in the past. Taking additional risk can increase the amount you'll eventually need to recover.

Recovery is a process.

PHASE 01

Stabilize

Protect housing, food, transportation and other essentials while stopping additional gambling losses.

PHASE 02

Inventory

List balances, minimum payments, APRs, income and essential expenses so you know what you're dealing with.

PHASE 03

Attack the debt

Choose a sustainable repayment strategy and direct additional money toward reducing expensive debt.

PHASE 04

Build protection

Create savings and safeguards that make another financial emergency less likely to restart the cycle.

PHASE 05

Measure progress

Track debt reduced and money retained instead of measuring success by wins and losses.

PHASE 06

Keep going

A financial setback can take time to repair. Progress still counts when it happens gradually.
