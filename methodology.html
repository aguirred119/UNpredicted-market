The process behind the probability.

Machine-learning classifier

The browser trains a two-feature logistic regression with standardized inputs, 600 full-batch gradient steps, learning rate 0.1 and L2 penalty 0.01 on feature weights. The earliest 80% of observations train the model. The remaining 20% are a chronological holdout. Standardization uses training data only.

The browser upload model is generic and experimental; its data is supplied by the visitor. You supply the dataset and define the features. Synthetic demo results are labeled and are not evidence of real performance.

Operator sports models

NBA and NFL research use licensed real historical results. Each model has a separate chronological training and held-out evaluation period, published coefficients and source checksums. The generic upload lab described above is separate from these operator models.

The NFL version 1 model trains on 2018–2023 and tests on 2024–2025. It uses current-season point margin and win fraction from up to eight prior games, at least four per team, with a 48-hour information lag. Regular season and home stadium games only. It estimates outright home wins, including overtime; a tie is a non-win. Injuries, quarterbacks, weather, roster changes and market prices are excluded.

Daily publication requires at least 300 NFL holdout games and a Brier improvement of 0.005 over the constant home-win baseline, fresh team history within 21 days, features within training ranges, and an exact match to current provider teams and kickoff. These thresholds permit experimental publication; they do not establish profitability or future accuracy. Inspect NFL evidence.

Simulation

Each trial samples an independent Bernoulli outcome from a fixed probability. A seeded Mulberry32 generator permits reproduction. The simulation report shows YES/NO counts and Monte Carlo standard error. This error describes sampling variation, not uncertainty about the real outcome probability. Repeating an arbitrary probability cannot validate it.

Limitations

Features may contain leakage; user confirmation does not detect it. Small samples, omitted variables, dependence, imbalance, selection effects and distribution change can invalidate a model. The lab does not implement confidence intervals for model parameters, automated feature discovery or execution-aware backtesting. Inputs outside the observed training range are flagged.

Evidence and results

Downloaded run records include timestamps, source labels, dataset hash where applicable, model parameters, holdout metrics, seed and trial counts. SHA-256 detects changes when compared with a trusted copy; it does not certify data truth or independently prove publication time. The local notebook is editable and cannot support a claim of audited public performance.

Public forecast publication

The operator publication workflow stamps predictions before the event. Forecasts include the event, selected outcome, settlement rule, source, input timestamp and analysis. Model forecasts must include reproducible logistic-regression parameters, features, chronological holdout evidence, dataset hash and simulation settings. Synthetic model records are rejected from the public production archive. These checks verify mathematical consistency, not the truth or quality of the dataset.

The versioned ledger chains hashes and appends separate win, loss or void records. Corrections reference the previous resolution; the original remains visible. Automated grading is prepared for completed US-sport match-winner results including overtime. Soccer, postponements and ambiguous results require reviewed resolutions. Pending records remain pending until there is sufficient evidence.

Model performance uses every resolved, non-void model forecast in the chosen view: Brier score and threshold accuracy at probability 0.5. Editorial selection hit rate is separate. Sample size, pending outcomes and voids are disclosed. No simulated return or profitability metric is claimed. Git timestamps and hashes are useful evidence but not independent auditing or immutable storage.

Market comparison

Bookmaker implied probability is 1 divided by decimal odds. It includes the bookmaker margin. The dashboard compares it with a published model probability only when the selected outcome matches. That difference excludes margin adjustments, execution costs and timing differences; it is not a certified profitable edge.

Advertising standard

We describe capabilities that actually exist. Manual runs are labeled assumptions. Trained runs are labeled machine-learning experiments. We do not claim guaranteed outcomes, demonstrated profitability or real-world accuracy from synthetic samples.

Descriptive form with visible source delays.

Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1 summaries use OpenFootball public-domain domestic league results. Each team’s window includes up to 10 same-season games. Wins, draws, losses, goals scored and goals allowed are descriptive statistics; no soccer AI/model forecast or simulation is claimed.

Community results can be delayed. The explorer and matchup pages show the last included source date separately from the snapshot generation time. Source dates have day precision. Results must be at least 48 hours after a conservative end-of-day UTC cutoff before inclusion; this is an availability assumption, not a confirmed publication timestamp.

Kickoff is supplied by the schedule provider; the result-source kickoff is not independently verified. Ambiguous matches, source errors and missing statistics remain unavailable or visibly dated. NFL and NBA: descriptive team statistics

Team Statistics shows all 32 NFL and 30 NBA teams, including teams without qualifying results. NFL form uses up to eight same-season regular-season games; NBA uses up to twenty. The record, points scored and allowed per game, and average point margin share the same window. The last five included results are shown separately. Empty current-season samples remain empty.

Both sources use a conservative 48-hour lag before snapshot generation. Snapshot retrieval time and the latest qualifying game date are shown separately. Revised source files are not archived point-in-time data. Preseason, playoff and neutral-site games are excluded. These are descriptive summaries, not model probabilities, spread forecasts or official standings.

Published editorial NFL/NBA point spreads can be graded from a unique source match with exact teams and kickoff time after the 48-hour lag. A manually reviewed final result may be appended earlier with its source and reasoning. A team winning outright does not mean its spread selection won. Results, including losses, are appended without altering the original pregame selection; corrections retain their history.

Soccer: three-outcome historical research

Five separate multinomial logistic models estimate home win, draw and away win. Training uses 2023–24; the untouched later-season test uses available 2024–25 results. Up to ten prior same-season games per team (minimum five) produce goal-margin difference and a combined goals-scored/allowed rate. Means and scales use training data only; 1,000 full-batch steps, learning rate 0.1 and L2 feature penalty 0.02 are fixed before testing. Coefficients stay frozen during the holdout.

Historical fixture dates have no verified kickoff. The cutoff starts at 00:00 UTC minus two hours on the fixture date, then subtracts 48 hours; result availability uses end-of-day UTC. This conservative assumption is not a verified result-publication time. Revised historical results and missing final scores are disclosed.

Multiclass Brier is the mean sum of squared errors across all three outcomes (0–2); the baseline uses three training-outcome frequencies. Accuracy, log loss and per-outcome calibration are reported without holdout tuning. These Brier scores cannot be compared directly with binary NBA/NFL scores. No current soccer predictions, simulations or profitability tests are included.
