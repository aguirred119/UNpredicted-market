Privacy

Effective October 2, 2026. This notice describes the research beta.

Data on your device

Uploaded training CSVs and trained model parameters remain in browser memory and are discarded when you leave or reload the page. Saved watchlists and notebook records use this browser's local storage. Saved runs may include your research question, input values, model parameters, dataset hash and evaluation metrics; raw training rows are not saved. Anyone with access to your browser profile may access these records.

Hosting

Vercel delivers the site and may process standard request information such as IP address, user agent and request time under its hosting policies. We have not added advertising trackers or third-party analytics. There are no accounts or payment collection in this beta. When you request sportsbook odds, our server sends the selected league to The Odds API. Your uploaded CSV, notebook and personal information are not sent to that provider.

Control

Use Delete local notebook to erase saved runs. Remove watched markets individually, or clear site storage in your browser to erase both. Exports are your responsibility to store securely. Avoid entering personal or confidential data.

Questions

For product or privacy questions, open a repository issue. Do not include private information in a public issue. An operator contact and applicable privacy-rights procedures must be established before cloud accounts launch.
