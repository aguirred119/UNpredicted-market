01 / TRAIN

Bring historical game data

CSV columns: date,feature_a,feature_b,outcome. Use 100–10,000 rows, unique chronological UTC timestamps, two numeric features and outcomes of 0 or 1. For sports, feature A could be the pregame rating difference and feature B the rest-day advantage; outcome 1 means the selected team won. Each feature must have been known before its outcome. Define how draws, overtime, and settlement rules are handled.

Synthetic demo data tests the software. It does not establish real-world accuracy.
