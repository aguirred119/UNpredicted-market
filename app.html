IPHONE / IPAD

Add with Safari.

  1. Open TONATI LAB in Safari. If you’re viewing it inside another app, open the link in Safari first.
  2. Open Safari’s menu if needed, then tap Share (the square with the upward arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. Scroll the sharing options if it isn’t visible.
  4. Keep Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.
  5. Open the TONATI LAB icon from your home screen.

ANDROID / CHROME

Add with your browser.

  1. Open TONATI LAB in Chrome.
  2. Use the Add TONATI LAB button above if your browser offers it.
  3. Otherwise, open Chrome’s menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then follow its confirmation.
  4. Open TONATI LAB from your home screen or app launcher.

Menu names and availability vary by browser. You can continue using the website at any time.
