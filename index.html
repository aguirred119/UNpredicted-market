You don't recover a loss by chasing it.

Recovery begins by stopping the cycle and dealing with what exists today.

STEP 01

Stop the bleeding

Don't try to win the money back. Remove access, deposits and other opportunities to chase previous losses.

STEP 02

Know the number

Write down every balance, interest rate and minimum payment. The number can be frightening, but it gives you something concrete to solve.

STEP 03

Start rebuilding

Create a sustainable repayment plan, protect essential expenses, and measure progress over months rather than bets.

If this feels bigger than money

Financial losses can bring panic, shame and an overwhelming urge to fix everything immediately. You don't have to handle that alone.

In the U.S., the National Problem Gambling Helpline can be reached by calling or texting 1-800-GAMBLER.

If you're in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 in the United States.
